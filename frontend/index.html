Frequently Asked Questions (AEO Q&A Knowledge Base)

What is ByteCollege and who is it designed for?

ByteCollege is a specialized academic companion engineered specifically for B.Tech Computer Science and Information Technology students enrolled at Hemvati Nandan Bahuguna Garhwal University (HNBGU). It provides verified course syllabi, past examination question papers (PYQs), real-time CGPA simulators, attendance monitors, and GATE preparation pathways.

How does ByteCollege calculate SGPA and CGPA for HNBGU engineering students?

ByteCollege implements HNBGU's official credit-weighted grading formula where Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is calculated as the sum of credit points multiplied by grade points earned, divided by total semester credits. The cumulative CGPA computes overall academic performance across all completed semesters.

Where can HNBGU engineering students download verified Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs)?

HNBGU engineering students can access and download authentic, verified End-Semester Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs) directly within ByteCollege's Syllabus and PYQs module, organized semester-by-semester from semester 1 to semester 8.
